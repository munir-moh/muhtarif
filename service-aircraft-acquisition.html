Services

Aircraft Acquisition, Pre-Purchase Inspection & Delivery Support

Aircraft acquisition is a high-value investment that requires robust technical due diligence and airworthiness assessment. We provide specialist aircraft acquisition and delivery support services, ensuring informed decision-making and a controlled transition from selection through to entry into service.

From aircraft pre-purchase inspection (PPI) to full delivery project management, our services are designed to protect your interests, minimise risk and ensure In-Service Date (ISD) targets are achieved.

We act as an independent technical partner—identifying hidden risks within aircraft technical records, maintenance status and airworthiness compliance before they impact your operation.

Aircraft Acquisition & Pre-Purchase Support

We provide comprehensive aircraft acquisition support services, including on-site inspections and technical analysis:

  • Aircraft Selection & Technical Evaluation: Assessment of aircraft suitability based on operational requirements, maintenance status and asset condition.
  • Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI) / Pre-Lease Inspection: Detailed aircraft physical inspection and technical records review, including airworthiness status, compliance status and maintenance history.
  • Technical Due Diligence: Identification of airworthiness risks, maintenance liabilities and upcoming cost drivers.
  • Airworthiness Review Support: Independent airworthiness assessment to support acquisition decisions and future Certificate of Airworthiness and/or Airworthiness Review Certificate planning.
  • Project Coordination & Vendor Liaison: Management of MROs and third-party providers to maintain acquisition timelines.

Aircraft Delivery & Lease Transition Management

We provide structured support for aircraft delivery, lease transitions and entry into service, ensuring continuity of airworthiness:

  • Delivery & Redelivery Project Management: Oversight of technical activities during aircraft acceptance or return conditions.
  • Bridging Maintenance & Work Package Control: Development and management of bridging checks and maintenance inputs to align with the receiving operator or CAMO.
  • Technical Records Review & Reconciliation: Full aircraft records audit, including back-to-birth traceability checks where required.
  • CAMO Transfer Support: Controlled transition between Continuing Airworthiness Management Organisations, ensuring no loss of compliance or data integrity.
  • Aircraft Registration & Deregistration Support: Coordination of technical and documentation requirements for change of ownership and registry.

Aircraft Import, Export & Change of Registry

Aircraft transitions between jurisdictions require careful management of regulatory compliance and certification requirements. We provide:

  • Aircraft Import & Export Certification Support: Assistance with aircraft acceptance onto new registers, including compliance with NAA requirements. End-to-end support for aircraft entering or leaving any NAA register, including documentation and airworthiness alignment.
  • Maintenance Programme (AMP) Alignment: Adjustment of the maintenance programme to meet applicable regulatory requirements in the receiving state.
  • Multi-Authority Liaison: Coordination with National Aviation Authorities to ensure a compliant and efficient transition.
