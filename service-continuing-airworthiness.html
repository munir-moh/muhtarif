Services

Continuing Airworthiness Management

As a fully independent Continuing Airworthiness Management Organisation (CAMO), we provide bespoke and customer-focused airworthiness management services tailored to your unique operational requirements.

As an independent CAMO, we deliver impartial aircraft airworthiness management with a clear focus on regulatory compliance, safety and long-term asset value protection.

Established over 15 years ago, our dedicated team delivers CAMO excellence across the commercial, leasing, business and special mission aviation sectors. We take pride in supporting our clients with secure and efficient fixed-wing or rotary-wing aircraft fleet management, ensuring you remain compliant at all times.

A Flexible Approach

Our airworthiness services are designed entirely around your specific requirements. We offer the flexibility to provide:

  • Project-Based Assistance: Targeted support for one-off projects or peak workloads.
  • Outsourced CAMO Support: Seamlessly acting as your dedicated continuing airworthiness department.
  • Regulatory Guidance: We can guide you through initial applications and complex regulatory filings.

Our Specialised Services

We provide a comprehensive range of CAMO airworthiness services, designed to ensure regulatory alignment and continued safety, always affiliating with your aircraft or helicopter specific regulatory requirements.

  • Aircraft Technical Log and Minimum Equipment List (MEL): Generation and management of Technical Logs and MEL documents to meet commercial and private operational requirements.
  • Maintenance Programme Management: Development, optimisation, amendment management and reviewing the continued effectiveness of maintenance programmes, all tailored to your specific operation.
  • Maintenance Planning & Component Management: Strategic management of scheduled maintenance and component replacement tasks, including the production of detailed work packages and work cards using our airworthiness management software systems.
  • Continuing Airworthiness Records: We ensure the "paper aircraft" and the "digital aircraft" is as airworthy as the physical one, through ongoing management of logbooks and log-cards, scanning, data back-up and storage of your continuing airworthiness records both hard copy and digital cloud based.
  • Life-Limited Part Tracking: Tracking component utilisation and service life limits to ensure ongoing compliance is maintained.
  • Mandatory and Non-Mandatory Information Management: Capture, assessment and management of compliance for Airworthiness Directives (ADs), Service Bulletins (SB's) and any other requirements applicable to your aircraft or helicopter.
  • Modifications and Repairs: Management of both Major and Minor modifications and repairs including liaison with Part 21 organisations.
  • Reliability Reporting: Detailed data capture, analysis and reporting to monitor fleet health and reliability. Assisting with repetitive defect monitoring to make informed decisions.
