Services

Airworthiness Review and Consultancy Services

Navigating the complexities of global aircraft certification requires more than just a checklist; it requires a specialised partner who understands the nuances of diverse regulatory environments and has direct experience working with aviation authorities.

We provide comprehensive airworthiness review and consultancy services, tailored to the unique demands of each operator and regulatory environment.

We take pride in facilitating comprehensive and efficient airworthiness reviews, ensuring your aircraft meet the highest standards of safety and compliance, regardless of where they are based. Whether reviews are required for Certificate of Airworthiness recommendations or renewals, or for UK registered aircraft, Airworthiness Review Certificate recommendations or extensions, we can help.

Bespoke Review Services

Our airworthiness review process is thorough, transparent and designed to fit your schedule. We offer:

  • Documented Airworthiness Reviews: Comprehensive review of aircraft technical records to verify completeness, accuracy and compliance with continuing airworthiness requirements.
  • Physical Aircraft Surveys: Inspection of the aircraft to confirm configuration, condition and alignment with approved data.
  • Findings Resolution Management: We don't just identify issues; we provide practical solutions and project management support to resolve findings efficiently.

Multi-Registry Expertise

Our dedicated team excels in technical management across a diverse range of jurisdictions. Our experience with leading regulatory bodies enables us to support your fleet wherever it is registered.

Our core competencies include:

  • Primary Approvals: Supporting aircraft registered across different regulatory environments and jurisdictions.
  • International Liaison: Experience navigating the specific requirements of international aircraft registries and aviation authorities.

Global Reach, Personal Focus

Whether you require project-based assistance for a single import or retained in-house support for a global leasing portfolio, our services are customer-focused. From the commercial, business and private aviation sectors we ensure your success through precision and technical integrity.

The Muhtarif Advantage

We combine the technical depth of an approved organisation with the agility of a bespoke consultancy. By acting as your advocate with National Aviation Authorities, we simplify the complex nature of the airworthiness review process and ensure your reviews are handled with professional rigor and a personal touch.
