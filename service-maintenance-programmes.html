Services

Customised Maintenance Programmes

A robust Aircraft Maintenance Programme (AMP) is fundamental to safe, compliant and cost-effective aircraft operation. We specialise in the development and management of customised maintenance programmes, tailored to your specific aircraft type, utilisation and operational environment.

Rather than relying on generic templates, we deliver bespoke AMP development aligned with your NAA requirements, ensuring your maintenance programme reflects how the aircraft is actually operated while maintaining full regulatory compliance.

Maintenance Programme Services

With extensive experience in fixed-wing and rotary-wing continuing airworthiness, we can provide a complete range of maintenance programme services:

  • AMP Development: Creation of maintenance programmes in accordance with regulatory requirements, including support for approval by the Competent Authority or by yourselves as a CAMO.
  • AMP Optimisation & Escalation: Controlled escalation/de-escalation of maintenance tasks based on operational data, OEM guidance and reliability programme data.
  • Annual Review & Effectiveness Assessment: Conduct periodic maintenance programme reviews to ensure ongoing suitability, efficiency and compliance.
  • AMP Amendment Management: Control of revisions and temporary variations in accordance with approved procedures and organisational privileges.
  • Airworthiness Limitations & Critical Task Control: Management of Airworthiness Limitation Items (ALI), CDCCL (Critical Design Configuration Control Limitations) and other operational related requirements.
  • Bridging Checks & Maintenance Programme Transitions: Development and control of bridging maintenance following aircraft import, lease transition or CAMO change, ensuring continuity of maintenance compliance between programmes.
  • Reliability & Risk-Based Analysis: Use of reliability data and risk-based assessment to support task optimisation while maintaining safety and regulatory compliance.

Precision Through Maintenance Programme Software

To ensure accuracy and control, we utilise dedicated, in-house maintenance programme management software, supporting:

  • Data Integrity & Configuration Control: A single, controlled dataset for all maintenance tasks, intervals and limitations.
  • Revision & Amendment Control: Structured management of revisions, ensuring alignment with OEM source data, Airworthiness Directives, Service Bulletins and operational or local requirements.
  • Regulatory Compliance Tracking: Full traceability of changes to support regulatory approval and audit requirements.

Delivering Compliance & Efficiency

Our approach ensures your aircraft or helicopter Maintenance Programme is:

  • Fully compliant with applicable regulations
  • Aligned with OEM and regulatory data
  • Adapted to your real-world operational profiles
  • Structured to minimise downtime and optimise maintenance cost

By translating complex technical and regulatory data into a clear and controlled maintenance programme, we support safe, efficient and predictable aircraft operation.

The Muhtarif Advantage

By combining our proprietary software with the specialised knowledge of our team, we offer a level of oversight that protects both your safety and your bottom line. We make sure your maintenance programme works for you.
