Services

Aircraft Technical Records Management & Storage

Effective aircraft technical records management is fundamental to maintaining continued airworthiness, regulatory compliance and asset value. We provide secure, structured solutions for the control, storage and management of fixed-wing and rotary-wing records and continuing airworthiness documentation.

Whether paper, digital or both, our solutions are designed around your operational and regulatory requirements. We ensure records are complete, traceable and readily accessible while supporting aircraft owners, operators and lessors with reliable technical records management.

Aircraft Records Management Services

We provide comprehensive aircraft technical records services:

  • Secure Records Storage: Controlled storage of original aircraft logbooks, technical records and supporting documentation in a secure and protected environment.
  • Aircraft Logbook & Records Updating: Accurate updating of airframe, engine and component records, ensuring chronological filing and regulatory compliance.
  • Technical Log & Flight Record Control: Monitoring and reconciliation of technical logs and flight data to maintain consistency with aircraft maintenance records.
  • Back-to-Birth Traceability Support: Verification and reconstruction of continuing airworthiness records, including component history, modifications, repairs and applicable compliance information.

Digital Records Management & Aircraft Records Digitisation

We provide digital aircraft records solutions that support secure, efficient and accessible information management:

  • Technical Records Scanning & Indexing: High-quality scanning and structured indexing of aircraft records for efficient retrieval.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): OCR-enabled records that support rapid searching and retrieval of maintenance and airworthiness data.
  • Secure Online Access: Controlled digital access to aircraft records through a secure online environment.
  • Digital Backup & Data Protection: Structured backup solutions designed to protect records and support data recovery.
  • Records Standardisation: Alignment of digital records with consistent industry and regulatory standards.

Records Audit, Recovery & Transition Support

We support aircraft transfers between operators, owners, CAMOs and airworthiness management systems through structured records assessment and management:

  • Aircraft Technical Records Audit: Detailed reviews of records to confirm completeness, accuracy and regulatory compliance.
  • Records Recovery & Reconstruction: Identification and recovery of missing or incomplete records using available historical information.
  • CAMO Transfer & Records Handover: Managed transition of technical records between operators or owners while maintaining continuity, presentation and data integrity.
  • Lease Transition & Redelivery Support: Preparation and organisation of records to meet applicable lessor return and audit requirements.

The Muhtarif Advantage

Your historical maintenance and airworthiness records are a vital asset. By combining secure physical storage with digital searchability, we provide the transparency and “smooth fleet transitions” that modern aviation demands. At Muhtarif Support, your data is always secure, searchable and audit-ready.
