About

What We Do

Airworthiness management tailored to your aircraft.

Established in 2009, we are a specialised and highly experienced team with over 15 years of operation, managing the continuing airworthiness requirements of a wide variety of aircraft types. Our expertise includes the management of both fixed wing and rotary aircraft including ATR, Boeing, BAE, Leonardo, Sikorsky, Embraer, MHI RJ Bombardier, Pilatus, Gulfstream, Cessna, Britten Norman, Beechcraft, Grob and many others.

We are recognised specialists in the BAE 146/Avro RJ aircraft, with unique and extensive knowledge gained from many combined years of dedicated CAMO support. Our services cover continuing airworthiness management, aircraft technical records, maintenance planning, airworthiness reviews, and pre purchase surveys.

In addition, we provide comprehensive data migration services, including the manipulation, analysis and restructuring of aircraft records and maintenance data. This ensures a seamless transition into your chosen systems with full accuracy, integrity and traceability.

How We Do It

A complete, bespoke range of services.

We take a customer focused approach, beginning with a detailed understanding of your operational needs and the history of your aircraft. By developing an in depth knowledge of how you operate, we deliver high quality, tailored support and comprehensive solutions that ensure the very best in airworthiness management.

Our data migration capability further enhances this approach, allowing us to clean, organise, analyse and transfer your technical records efficiently and securely supporting smooth transitions, improved data quality, integrity and maintaining longterm compliance.

What Sets Us Apart

We put you first. We tailor everything to you.

At AATS, attention to detail is central to our service. We listen, we advise and we create bespoke solutions that align with your regulatory requirements and operational objectives. Our specialised team is committed to delivering professional, cost effective support to operators and owners worldwide.

We are here to make airworthiness management easier for you—ensuring your aircraft remain compliant, your records remain accurate and you have complete peace of mind that your continuing airworthiness needs are expertly managed.
